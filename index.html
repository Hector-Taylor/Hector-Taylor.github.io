About

I am a researcher and builder in Zurich.

I am a PhD candidate in the Psychedelic Research and Therapy Development group at the University Hospital of Psychiatry Zurich / University of Zurich. My work sits at the intersection of psychedelic science, virtual reality, embodiment and chronic pain.

I am interested in how altered states change the way people infer, own and act through their bodies, and how immersive environments can be used to measure and potentially reshape that process. This site is my canonical home online for research updates, project notes, writing, publications, tools and experiments as they become ready to share.

Research

From measurement to mechanism to translation.

  • Pain and body-specific perception

    I study whether pain changes perception in a body-specific way, rather than merely making all sensory judgments noisier or more difficult.

  • Psilocybin, VR and embodied self-binding

    I use immersive VR to test how psilocybin may alter embodied self-binding, especially when visual and proprioceptive body signals conflict.

  • Embodied VR for chronic pain translation

    I am helping develop the clinical translation of this work toward chronic-pain populations, asking whether embodied VR can be designed as more than a distraction tool: as a way to retune maladaptive body representation over time.

Projects

Projects connect research, method and public culture.

  • State x World x Task

    A methodological framework for matching induced modes of consciousness with designed virtual worlds and specific therapeutic or experimental tasks. Safety and support conditions are part of the method, not an afterthought.

  • PROZ

    Through PROZ, I help support public-facing psychedelic-science programming and community infrastructure in Zurich: speaker events, serious research conversations and the organizational systems that help the group work coherently.

  • Hexocortex and tools

    I am building a personal research operating system for turning papers, transcripts, code, meetings and rough notes into durable project knowledge. Small public tools and experiments, including Shader Lab, will live here as they are ready.
